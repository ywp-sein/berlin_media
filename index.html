WHY STEPPING STONES?

A story is never
just a single moment.

Decades ago, stumbling stones were placed throughout Germany to help us remember—to keep the stories of the past present as we move forward.

Today, we want to place stepping stones of hope throughout our city. Each one points to a journey: a trajectory of grace unfolding through people, places, encounters, and generations.

Together, these testimonies remind us: God is still speaking. God is still at work. God is still here. They help us recognize God's presence, find hope for today, and take the next step forward.
